Player

Hakuro follows one player at a time. YouTube Music is read from the Windows media session, so it needs no sign-in: play it in your browser or in the desktop app and Hakuro follows along.

Copy the Client ID from your Spotify Developer app. Set its redirect URI to http://127.0.0.1:8787/callback. No Client Secret is needed.

Lyrics search order

Move sources up or down and select which ones to search. A song's preferred source takes priority.

    Compact mode always keeps room for three lines: the line being sung, and the one on either side.
